## Case Study 1: Series B Fintech, 60 employees

**Segment:** Recently-funded startup (Segment 1)
**Engagement:** Managed talent outsourcing — 6 Python data engineers, 12 months

**Situation:** Closed $18M Series B. Needed to build a real-time fraud detection pipeline. Internal recruiting was producing 1 hire per month; they needed 6 in 8 weeks.

**What Tenacious delivered:**
- 6 mid-to-senior Python engineers onboarded in 3 weeks
- Engineers operated under Tenacious delivery lead, integrated into client's sprint cadence
- Fraud detection pipeline shipped to production in 4 months

**Outcomes:**
- Time-to-team: 3 weeks vs. projected 6 months via in-house hiring
- Pipeline processing 2.3M transactions/day within 6 months
- Client renewed for a second year, expanded to 9 engineers

**ACV:** ~$430K (year 1)

## Case Study 2: Mid-Market SaaS Platform, 800 employees

**Segment:** Restructuring cost (Segment 2)
**Engagement:** Managed talent outsourcing — 10 engineers (Go + infrastructure), 18 months

**Situation:** Post-layoff (cut 15% of engineering). Board pressure to reduce burn by 30% without reducing feature velocity. Needed to replace 10 US-based engineers with an offshore team at lower cost.

**What Tenacious delivered:**
- 10 engineers (4 Go, 3 infrastructure, 3 data) ramped in 4 weeks
- Tenacious delivery lead managed the team and reported directly to client's VP Engineering
- Zero feature velocity drop measured over first two quarters

**Outcomes:**
- Engineering cost reduced 47% for the replaced roles
- Feature velocity maintained at 98% of pre-layoff baseline (measured by story points shipped)
- Board cited the restructuring as "operationally disciplined" in next earnings call

**ACV:** ~$680K (year 1)

## Case Study 3: Series C AI Company, 200 employees

**Segment:** Specialized capability gap (Segment 4)
**Engagement:** Project-based consulting — ML platform migration, 14 weeks

**Situation:** Migrating from a legacy ML training pipeline (custom Kubernetes jobs) to a modern MLOps stack (Ray + MLflow + Weights & Biases). Internal ML team had model expertise but not infrastructure expertise.

**What Tenacious delivered:**
- 4 ML infrastructure engineers (2 senior, 1 staff, 1 mid) for 14 weeks
- Migrated 23 training pipelines to Ray
- Set up MLflow experiment tracking and W&B integration
- Handed off with documentation and 2 weeks of shadowing

**Outcomes:**
- Training pipeline runtime reduced 62% (GPU utilization improved from 34% to 78%)
- Model iteration cycle dropped from 2 weeks to 3 days
- Client's internal team fully self-sufficient after handoff

**ACV:** ~$210K (one-time project)
