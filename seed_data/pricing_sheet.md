# Tenacious Consulting & Outsourcing — Pricing Sheet (Public Tier)

## Managed Talent Outsourcing

| Role Level | Monthly Rate (per engineer) | Typical Engagement |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-level Engineer (3–5 yrs) | $4,000–$6,000 | 6–24 months |
| Senior Engineer (5–8 yrs) | $6,000–$9,000 | 6–24 months |
| Staff/Lead Engineer (8+ yrs) | $9,000–$13,000 | 6–18 months |
| Engineering Manager | $10,000–$14,000 | 6–12 months |

### Engagement Sizing
- Minimum team: 3 engineers
- Typical team: 3–12 engineers
- Ramp time: 2–4 weeks for first engineers, 1–2 weeks for additions
- Management overhead: included (Tenacious delivery lead manages the team)

### ACV Ranges by Segment
- Segment 1 (Recently funded): $240K–$720K
- Segment 2 (Restructuring): $360K–$720K (larger teams, longer engagements)
- Segment 3 (Leadership transition): $240K–$540K
- Segment 4 (Capability gap): see Project Consulting below

### ACV Range (Project Consulting)
- $80K–$300K per engagement

## Pricing Rules for the Agent

1. The agent MAY quote the ranges above in outreach and qualification
2. The agent MUST NOT negotiate pricing or offer discounts
3. Any request for custom pricing, volume discounts, or detailed SOW pricing MUST route to a human Tenacious delivery lead
4. The agent should frame pricing as "typical ranges" not "your price will be"
5. If a prospect asks "how much?", respond with the relevant range and offer to discuss specifics on a discovery call
