# Tenacious Consulting & Outsourcing — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Segment 1: Recently-Funded Series A/B Startups

**Description:** Companies that closed a $5–30M round in the last 6 months. Typically 15–80 people. Hiring velocity outstripping recruiting capacity.

**Why they buy:** Need to scale engineering output faster than in-house hiring can support. Budget is fresh and runway is the clock.

### Qualifying Filters
- Funding round: Series A or Series B
- Round size: $5M–$30M
- Recency: closed within last 180 days
- Employee count: 15–80
- Hiring signal: open engineering roles increasing over 60 days
- Industry: B2B technology, SaaS, data/AI, fintech, healthtech, devtools

### Disqualifying Filters
- Layoff in last 120 days (route to Segment 2 instead)
- No engineering roles open (no scaling signal)
- Consumer-only company with no B2B component
- Company already has 100+ engineers (not early-stage scaling pain)

### Pitch Angle
- "Scale your engineering team in weeks, not quarters"
- At high AI maturity: "Scale your AI team faster than in-house hiring can support"
- At low AI maturity: "Stand up your first AI function with a dedicated squad"

## Segment 2: Mid-Market Platforms Restructuring Cost

**Description:** Public or late-stage companies (200–2,000 people) under pressure to cut burn without cutting output. Often post-layoff or post-restructure.

**Why they buy:** Replace higher-cost roles with offshore equivalents; keep delivery capacity; quiet signal of operational discipline to the board.

### Qualifying Filters
- Employee count: 200–2,000
- Layoff event in last 120 days OR public cost-cutting announcement
- Still has active engineering job posts (maintaining output)
- Stage: Series C+, public, or PE-backed
- Revenue: $20M+ ARR or equivalent

### Disqualifying Filters
- Company in active shutdown/wind-down
- No remaining engineering hiring (fully contracted)
- Recently funded Series A/B (route to Segment 1)

### Pitch Angle
- "Maintain delivery velocity at 40–60% lower cost per engineer"
- At high AI maturity: "Preserve your AI capability while restructuring cost"
- At low AI maturity: "Right-size your engineering spend without losing ground"

---

## Segment 3: Engineering-Leadership Transitions

**Description:** Companies with a new CTO or VP Engineering appointed in the last 90 days.

**Why they buy:** New leaders routinely reassess vendor contracts and offshore mix in their first 6 months. This is a narrow but high-conversion window.

### Qualifying Filters
- New CTO, VP Engineering, or Head of Engineering appointed within 90 days
- Company size: 50+ employees
- Has existing engineering team (not a solo technical hire)
- B2B technology company

### Disqualifying Filters
- Leadership change is a demotion/interim (not a strategic hire)
- Company under 30 employees (too small for outsourcing)
- New leader is a co-founder returning (different dynamic)

### Pitch Angle
- "New engineering leaders typically reassess their vendor and offshore strategy in the first 90 days — here's what the data shows for companies at your stage"
- AI maturity does NOT gate this segment — the new leader's own stance is the variable

---

## Segment 4: Specialized Capability Gaps

**Description:** Companies attempting a specific build — ML platform migration, agentic systems, data contracts — where in-house skills do not match the need.

**Why they buy:** Project-based consulting, not outsourcing. Higher margin, shorter commitment, portfolio value.

### Qualifying Filters
- AI maturity score: 2 or above (HARD GATE)
- Specific capability gap identifiable from job posts or public signals
- Gap matches Tenacious bench capabilities (Python, Go, data, ML, infra)
- Project-scoped need (not ongoing staffing)

### Disqualifying Filters
- AI maturity score 0 or 1 (do NOT pitch Segment 4)
- Gap does not match Tenacious bench availability
- Company has already hired for the capability (gap closed)

### Pitch Angle
- "Your team is building [specific capability] — our bench has [N] engineers with production experience in exactly that stack"
- Always reference bench availability; never over-commit

---

## Cross-Segment Rules

1. A company can match multiple segments. Priority: Segment 3 > Segment 4 > Segment 1 > Segment 2
2. If confidence in segment assignment is below 0.6, send generic exploratory email
3. Post-layoff companies are NEVER Segment 1, even if recently funded
4. Bench-to-brief match is required before any capacity claim
5. AI maturity < 2 blocks Segment 4 pitches entirely
